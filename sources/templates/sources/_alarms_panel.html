{% comment %} Pipeline alarms — deliberately a second table, not more columns on the first one. Four of the threshold table's eleven columns (Context, Measure, Window, and the chart link that depends on both) have no meaning for a rule that watches ETL state rather than fact rows, and rendering them empty would suggest they could be filled in. The two tables share everything that is genuinely shared: the recipient machinery, the channel checkboxes, the severity select, rowFields and the same edit/save cycle. {% endcomment %}
