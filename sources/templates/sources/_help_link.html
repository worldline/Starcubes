Opens in a new tab on purpose: the operator is usually mid-configuration and should not lose the form they are filling in. Put it INSIDE the .card-title span, not after it: .card-header is a flex row with space-between, so a sibling gets pushed to the far edge on a card whose header holds nothing else, and lands somewhere in the middle on one that does. Inside the title it sits in the same place on every card. The styles live in sources/base.html next to .card-header, because that is where the alignment they depend on is decided. {% endcomment %}
