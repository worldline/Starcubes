Measures Chart measure dropdown. agg is count(*) or func(measure column). multiline draws one line per group series, multipoint one dot per slot per series; multistep draws multiline as a staircase, each bin holding its value across the bin it represents; multibar stacks them. The five ladder styles (quantile-bands, quantile-steps, quantile-steps-open, error-bars, candlestick) draw a whole array per bin instead of one value, and two agg forms feed them. quantile(col) with a percentile ladder (e.g. 5,25,50,75,95) works with all five, and always reads the fact table — percentiles do not fold. extent(col) with a min,avg,max ladder (or just min,max) draws a band around the mean and works with quantile-bands/quantile-steps/quantile-steps-open only; it can be served from a rollup, so it stays fast at coarse resolutions and keeps working below the retention floor. Within each form the renderer is the only difference — quantile-steps connects the rungs with risers (default), quantile-steps-open drops them; error-bars marks each pair symmetric around the median; candlestick draws a boxplot (outer pair = whiskers, inner pair = body, median tick).
